STACKING EXAMPLE
----------------

  Shape s = factory.createShape("CIRCLE", color);
  s = new FillDecorator(s, AppColor.RED);
  s = new BorderDecorator(s, AppColor.BLACK, 2.0);
  s = new ShadowDecorator(s, AppColor.GRAY, 3);
  s.draw(gc);

  Execution order: ShadowDecorator
                      └─► BorderDecorator
                               └─► FillDecorator
                                        └─► CircleShape.draw(gc)


====================================================================
FULL PATTERN RELATIONSHIP SUMMARY
====================================================================

  AppState (Singleton)
    ├── holds current color, brush size, observer list
    ├── notifies ──────────────────────────────────────► IObserver
    │                                              (Canvas, UIPanel)
    └── uses ──────────────────────────────────────────► ToolManager

  ToolManager (Singleton)
    ├── holds current DrawStrategy
    ├── uses ──────────────────────────────────────────► DrawStrategy
    └── uses ──────────────────────────────────────────► CommandHistory

  DrawStrategy (Strategy)
    ├── FreehandStrategy   ── draws free lines
    ├── ShapeStrategy      ── uses ShapeFactory to build shapes
    └── EraserStrategy     ── clears canvas area

  ShapeFactory (Factory Method)
    └── creates ───────────────────────────────────────► Shape
                                          (Circle / Rect / Line)

  ShapeDecorator (Decorator)
    ├── FillDecorator      ── adds fill
    ├── BorderDecorator    ── adds border
    └── ShadowDecorator    ── adds shadow

  ICommand (Command)
    ├── DrawCommand        ── adds shape to canvas
    ├── DeleteCommand      ── removes shape from canvas
    └── ClearCommand       ── saves Memento then clears canvas

  CommandHistory (Command – Invoker)
    └── manages Stack<ICommand> for undo / redo

  CanvasMemento (Memento)
    └── snapshot of shapes list at a point in time

  CanvasCaretaker (Memento – Caretaker)
    └── manages Stack<CanvasMemento>

  Canvas
    ├── implements IObserver
    ├── acts as Originator (creates CanvasMemento)
    └── restored by ClearCommand via CanvasCaretaker
